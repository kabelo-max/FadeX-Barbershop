About FadeX Barbershop

FadeX Barbershop was established in 2022 to provide professional, modern and affordable grooming services to the local community.

Our barbershop provides quality haircuts and grooming services in a friendly and professional environment.

Our Mission

To provide professional haircuts and grooming services at affordable prices while delivering excellent customer service to every client.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's most trusted and recognised modern barbershop brands through quality service, innovation and customer satisfaction.

Who We Serve

  • Men looking for professional grooming services
  • Teenagers and students
  • Working professionals
  • Parents looking for quality haircuts for their children
